## The 15-minute SOP

**1. Map.** Write the real steps in order, including the ones you do "automatically."
If you can't map it, you can't document it — do the task once while writing each step down.

**2. Document.** One procedure, one SOP, under 2 pages:

- **Purpose** — one sentence: why it exists, what "done right" looks like.
- **Steps** — numbered, each starting with a verb.
- **Quality check** — how to verify the output is correct; the common mistake.
- **Exceptions** — when judgment overrides the procedure → who to escalate to.
- **Owner / review date** — who owns it, review every 90 days.

**3. Hand off.** Owner + deadline + written definition of done. Complete only when
the owner can execute without follow-up calls.

**4. Audit.** Quarterly: which SOPs are stale, missing, or ignored? Fix the SOP or
the process — don't just re-send it.

**Output:**

```
### SOP: [TASK NAME]
owner: / review every: 90 days

### PURPOSE
one sentence

### STEPS
1. [Verb] ...

### QUALITY CHECK
- [ ] verification step

### EXCEPTIONS
when judgment overrides → escalate to [who]

### HANDOFF
owner: / deadline: / definition of done:
```
